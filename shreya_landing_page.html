What I've Built

Projects

Hardware · SIH Hackathon

Alcohol Detection Auto-Cut Circuit

A hardware safety device for drivers. It senses alcohol in the driver's breath and automatically disconnects the ignition if the detected level crosses the legal limit. Built and presented during Smart India Hackathon in my first year. The goal was simple — stop the car before the accident happens.

Software · Field Project

Plant Disease Detection Software

A software tool that lets farmers upload a photo of a plant leaf and get back information about whether the plant is diseased and what the likely issue is. Built using image processing techniques. The idea came from wanting to make early disease detection accessible to farmers who may not have expert help nearby.
